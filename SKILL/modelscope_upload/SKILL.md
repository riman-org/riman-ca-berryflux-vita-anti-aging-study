# RIMAN RESEARCH: Clinical & Microclimate Evaluation of Berryflux Vita (Raspberry Plant Cell Culture) in the Canadian Market

## 简介

背景：本研究旨在评估 RIMAN 力曼旗下专利活性成分 Berryflux Vita（源自覆盆子植物细胞培养）在加拿大多样气候区的功效、质地表现及消费者转化指标。方法：对蒙特利尔、多伦多和温哥华的 200 名参与者进行分层调查，使用李克特量表评估保湿、弹性改善及加元定价敏感度，并结合净推荐值（NPS）分析。结果：整体综合平均分为 3.49，前两档百分比（Top-2-Box）为 48.7%。值得注意的是，产品在低湿度环境下的吸收速度获得高分（平均 3.88，Top-2-Box 85.5%），但两周后感知的弹性改善较为温和（平均 3.28）。严寒冬季触发了最高的支付意愿（98.0%）。战略启示：尽管从试用装到正装套装的转化率高达 57.5%，但负面的 NPS（-24.5）凸显了初始满意度与长期推荐之间的巨大落差，主要源于对功效声明的不确定性（95.5% 障碍率）。本报告为优化力曼官网区域策略及增强对 INCELLDERM 技术的信任提供了可执行的洞察。

## English overview

Background: This study evaluates the efficacy, texture performance, and consumer conversion metrics of RIMAN Skincare's proprietary active, Berryflux Vita (derived from raspberry plant cell culture), within the diverse climatic zones of Canada. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 participants across Montreal, Toronto, and Vancouver assessed Likert-scale perceptions of moisture retention, elasticity improvement, and price sensitivity in CAD, alongside Net Promoter Score (NPS) analysis. Results: The overall composite mean was 3.49 with a Top-2-Box percentage of 48.7%. Notably, texture absorption in low-humidity environments scored high (Mean 3.88, Top-2-Box 85.5%), while perceived elasticity gains after two weeks remained moderate (Mean 3.28).
