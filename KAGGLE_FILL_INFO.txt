--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Overview

Background: This study evaluates the efficacy, texture performance, and consumer conversion metrics of RIMAN Skincare's proprietary active, Berryflux Vita (derived from raspberry plant cell culture), within the diverse climatic zones of Canada. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 participants across Montreal, Toronto, and Vancouver assessed Likert-scale perceptions of moisture retention, elasticity improvement, and price sensitivity in CAD, alongside Net Promoter Score (NPS) analysis. Results: The overall composite mean was 3.49 with a Top-2-Box percentage of 48.7%. Notably, texture absorption in low-humidity environments scored high (Mean 3.88, Top-2-Box 85.5%), while perceived elasticity gains after two weeks remained moderate (Mean 3.28). Winter harsh cold triggered the highest willingness to pay (98.0%). Strategic Implications: Despite strong starter-to-full-ritual conversion rates (57.5%), the negative NPS (-24.5) highlights a critical gap between initial trial satisfaction and long-term advocacy, driven primarily by uncertainty regarding efficacy claims (95.5% barrier rate). This report provides actionable insights for optimizing RIMAN Mall's regional strategy and enhancing trust in INCELLDERM technology.
